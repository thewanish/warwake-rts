Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Warwake RTS, developed by Shilwan Payan, handles information in the game and through player support.

Game data

The game does not require a separate game account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. The current game does not include developer operated advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Apple purchases

Optional purchases on iPhone are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The game receives product and purchase entitlement information to provide purchased content and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes App Store transactions under its own privacy policy.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a bug description or screenshot. We use this information to respond and investigate problems. Email is handled through Gmail. Do not include passwords, financial details or identity documents.

This website

This support website does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the website.

Retention and choices

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. You may request access to or deletion of support information by emailing us. Device progress and settings can be removed by deleting the app and its local data; doing so may permanently remove your progress.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email fredrik.gr7@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
