How do I unlock Kalhor?

Complete all three planets to earn the faction, or choose the optional permanent Kalhor purchase in the game.

I purchased Kalhor but it is locked.

Use the game's Restore Purchases option while signed into the same Apple account used for the purchase. If the purchase is pending, wait for Apple to complete it. Contact support if the issue continues.

How do I send bug reports?

Email your bug report to fredrik.gr7@gmail.com.

The game runs slowly.

Try normal battle speed and close other demanding apps. If performance remains poor, send support your device model, mission, day and selected battle speed.

Do I need a game account?

No separate game account is required to play Warwake RTS. Purchases on iPhone use your Apple account.
